## Overall Narrative

The presentation argues that the project responds to the brief by turning publication into an interactive system. The final form is not a static book: it is a web-based reading interface where reader input changes a living Earth state, branches the story, and produces an ending card. The deck moves from brief interpretation, to source/content transformation, to research evidence, to interface mechanics, to prototype testing, to final deliverables and reflection.

The central project title is **The Earth, Written by Us**. The first slide uses the brief title **Publishing Forms: Radical Earth** because it frames the project within the assignment.
